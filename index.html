THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

Teaching technology.
Learning every day.

I'm Ritesh Kumar, a computer teacher with a strong interest in technology, digital education and the creative possibilities of the web. I enjoy making computer concepts easier to understand and helping learners build confidence through clear explanations and practical examples.

My journey brings together classroom teaching and continuous self-learning. Alongside my work at Mantra Computer Institute in Muzaffarpur, I am currently studying Computer Science and strengthening my understanding of programming, productivity tools and modern digital workflows.

I believe technology becomes meaningful when people can use it to solve real problems. Whether I am preparing a lesson, exploring a new tool or building a small web project, my approach stays the same: stay curious, practice consistently and keep improving.
